Welcome to

Gains Atlas

A gym aid for building specific muscles. Tap a body part, get every movement for it with the sets, reps, rest, and the mistake to avoid.

It is a reference, not a coach: it won't write your programme or check your form.

How it works

  1. Tap a muscle on the body. Switch Front/Back for the other side.
  2. Pick a movement. Open it for the step-by-step and the illustration.
  3. Filter by anywhere, home or gym, and by the kit you actually have.

23 body parts, 138 movements — including the joints and grip work people forget.

Install it as an app

Gains Atlas is a web app. It installs to your home screen and works offline after the first load. No app store, no account.

You're using the installed app. Nothing more to do.

  • Android, Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen”.
  • iPhone or iPad: it must be Safari, not Chrome. Tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
